Enter or the yellow button: 3-second countdown, then records for 3 seconds (best for two-handed signs). Hold Space to record while held. ↑ ↓ change sign. For each sign, press Enter about 3 times, lowering your hand and signing again between each (a bit closer, further, or turned). Separate attempts teach the model far more than one long recording.
